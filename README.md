### 🛠️ What I do

10+ years of WordPress: busy sites, multi-site setups, headless migrations and online stores. That's where I learned that people depend on what you ship.

These days I'm a bit obsessive about AI agents. They're fast. The bugs show up later, in review. I'd rather find them before that, so I make my agents plan and check their work first. It's the part of the work I enjoy most right now, and where I want to spend more of my time.
